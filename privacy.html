Sage Studio

Privacy Policy · last updated 6 November 2026

What Sage Studio is

Sage Studio is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel Sage (@sagesoreal). It uploads the owner's own animated films to that channel, fills in their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, schedules when they go public, and reads the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube API Services

Sage Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it uses

Only data about the owner's own channel: its videos, their metadata, playlists, and analytics (views, watch time, retention, click-through rate). It does not access other people's channels, comments, contacts or any personal data of viewers.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere except back to YouTube through its official API. Statistics are kept for as long as the owner keeps them for reviewing the channel, and can be deleted at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Removing it stops the tool immediately.
